## Number->name source: shirtNumber, NOT jerseyNumber (contradicts the task premise)

The recognizer reads the physical back-of-shirt number. In the oracle parquet that is **`shirtNumber`**, not `jerseyNumber` -- the two disagree for 12 players, e.g.:

| player | jerseyNumber | shirtNumber (back) |
|---|---|---|
| Trent Alexander-Arnold | 12 | 66 |
| Luis Díaz | 14 | 7 |
| Darwin Núñez | 7 | 9 |
| Conor Bradley | 12 | 84 |
| Caoimhin Kelleher | 1 | 62 |
| Jarell Quansah | 4 | 78 |
| Harry Maguire | 12 | 5 |
| Diogo Dalot | 2 | 20 |

Decisive case: our verified `20` back-reads are Diogo Dalot (shirtNumber 20); no Man Utd player has jerseyNumber 20 (Dalot's jerseyNumber is 2), so under `jerseyNumber` every verified Man Utd `20` anchor would be **unmatchable**. This module uses `shirtNumber` and cross-checks `jerseyNumber` only to quantify the gap.

## Honest caveats

- **Hero-shot concentration:** ~5-6 distinct back-numbers surface legible close-up reads this match; #8 (Bruno) is 82% of anchors. This names the players who get repeated close-up hero shots, not a uniform XI.

- **ReID is the bottleneck, not anchor precision.** OSNet is kit-dominated (Stage-2a: median cosine 0.81 on same-team pairs; 35% merge precision), so the margin gate rejects most same-kit disambiguations -- that is why attachment yield (392) is far below the anchor count (6322). Anchor *read* precision is ~99%; carrying reads onto the right *track* is the hard, unsolved half.

- **Season-scale extrapolation:** at ~5-6 hero-shot players/match, a 38-match season yields close-up anchors concentrated on the same marquee names (Bruno, Rashford, ...). Close-up anchors **supplement** roster/relink priors for those players; uniform per-player naming still needs the cluster/VLM close-up reader (Sem 2).
