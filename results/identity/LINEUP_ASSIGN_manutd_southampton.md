What the layer genuinely adds beyond open-set naming: (1) a principled per-player **confidence** from vote mass + position agreement; (2) explicit **coverage accounting** of the full 22-plus roster with reasons for each abstention; (3) **substitution-window** enforcement (a sub's number cannot bind before entry); (4) a **GK veto** guarding keeper<->outfield swaps; (5) abstention so nothing is forced.
