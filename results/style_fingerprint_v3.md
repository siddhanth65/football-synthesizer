# Style fingerprint v3 (Plan B-4) - tracking-native, full 6-match corpus

Three deterministic primitives over player positions + the proximity possession proxy, run
on the processed Man Utd matches. No learned model, no pass stream. Generated by
`tools/run_style_fingerprint.py`; engine `fingerprint/style_fingerprint.py`. v1 (n=3) and
v2 (n=5, southampton excluded) are kept alongside at `results/style_fingerprint_v1.md` /
`results/style_fingerprint_v2.md`.

**Corpus: all 6 processed matches.** `southampton_manutd` was excluded at v2 (team anchor
collapse, balance 0.004) but has since been re-anchored (balance 0.909: 67532 team-0 vs
61404 team-1 player rows), so it now yields two usable sides and joins the corpus. All six
(brighton, liverpool, fulham, palace, tottenham, southampton) are balanced 0.90-1.00. 12
team-match sides.

Config: SW_K=24 directions, SW_Q=12 quantiles, 64 k-means prototypes/side, transition window 5s, pressure radius 4.57m.

## Honest read (10 lines)

1. Identity with southampton in (full n=6): the territorial signal has essentially COLLAPSED. Mean
   intra-ManU distance 5.347 vs ManU-to-opponent 5.435 -- a 0.09 gap, down from 0.74 at n=5 (4.73 vs
   5.47) and 2.0 at n=3 (4.36 vs 6.38). The gap shrinks monotonically as matches are added; at the
   full corpus it is negligible. The tidy "every ManU side nearest another ManU side" verdict of v1
   does not survive.
2. Only 3 of 6 ManU sides now find another ManU side nearest (vLiv->vFul, vFul->vLiv, vTot->vLiv).
   The other three point at OPPONENTS: vBri->Southampton, vPal->Crystal Palace, vSou->Fulham. The
   self-clustering that was 4/5 at n=5 is now a minority.
3. Southampton is WHY the signal drops. In the away 0-3 win Man Utd played an outlier shape: in-
   possession build-up 37.0 m and deepest line 31.7 m -- ~15 m lower than any other ManU side (they
   build up in the 52-58 m band in every other match). So ManU_vSou's nearest neighbour is an opponent
   (Fulham), and it even inverts the one v2 habit: here the in-possession line (37) sat LOWER than the
   out-of-possession line (43), the reverse of the get-up-the-pitch pattern that held in the other
   five. A deep counter-attacking away win looks nothing like the home games.
4. Still NO shape fingerprint (centred). Remove the centroid (3b) and intra 1.582 vs cross 1.679 --
   essentially unchanged from v2 (1.558 vs 1.684) and no separation. What little repeats is territory
   (where they stand), and even that is now marginal; formation shape shows nothing.
5. The "consistent high build-up in possession" trait holds in 5 of 6 (in_poss build-up 52-58 m) but
   NOT in the Southampton away win (37 m). It is a home / positive-game-state habit, not an invariant
   -- Southampton is the counter-example.
6. Counter-press match-level now spans 0.46-0.75 across the six (ManU_vSou 0.460 is a new low, below
   ManU_vTot 0.479; ManU_vPal 0.750 the high). Not a fixed signature -- and crucially the two WINS
   split the range at level state (Fulham high, Southampton low), so match-level press does not track
   result: see `results/WINS_VS_LOSSES.md` for the by-state synthesis.
7. Palace 0-0 texture unchanged: ManU pressed hardest of all (0.750) while Palace barely counter-
   pressed (0.314, regain 0.086) and lost the ball outside their third fewest of any side (35).
8. Liverpool 0-3 distortion unchanged at n=6: the win-it-and-go attacking transition ran shallow vs
   Liverpool (trans_pos build-up 48.9 m vs 60.8 Brighton / 56.9 Fulham) and ManU sat lower and
   narrower in possession -- beaten in the win-it/lose-it phase, not out-shaped.
9. Ceilings unchanged: possession + counter-press rest on 1.6-2.7k proximity-possession samples and
   35-103 outside-third losses per side (ball-gap), and the partial broadcast view inflates absolute
   line heights ~+11m: compare across matches, not to FIFA numbers.
10. Verdict at full n=6: the territorial ManU signature that looked clean at n=3 does NOT survive the
    complete corpus -- intra-vs-cross gap 0.09, only 3/6 self-nearest, and the away win is an outright
    outlier. No shape fingerprint, no stable press. A single-team "style fingerprint" from six
    broadcast matches is dominated by opponent / game-state / territory, not a durable identity.
