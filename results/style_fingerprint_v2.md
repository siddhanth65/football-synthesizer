# Style fingerprint v2 (Plan B-4) - tracking-native, 6-match corpus

Three deterministic primitives over player positions + the proximity possession proxy, run
on the processed Man Utd matches. No learned model, no pass stream. Generated by
`tools/run_style_fingerprint.py`; engine `fingerprint/style_fingerprint.py`. v1 (n=3) is
kept alongside at `results/style_fingerprint_v1.md`.

**Corpus: 5 of the 6 processed matches.** `southampton_manutd` is excluded -- its team anchor
collapsed (149084 team-0 vs 670 team-1 player rows, balance 0.004), leaving one meaningless
22-player-blob side; it needs re-anchoring in the generator before it can enter two-team
analytics. The five here (brighton, liverpool, fulham, palace, tottenham) are balanced
0.92-1.00. 10 team-match sides.

Config: SW_K=24 directions, SW_Q=12 quantiles, 64 k-means prototypes/side, transition window 5s, pressure radius 4.57m.

## Honest read (10 lines)

1. Identity at n=6 (5 usable): WEAKER than n=3, no longer a clean split. In the territory+shape
   embedding (3a) 4 of 5 ManU sides still find another ManU side nearest (vBri->vLiv, vLiv->vFul,
   vFul->vLiv, vTot->vLiv), and mean intra-ManU 4.73 stays below ManU-to-opponent 5.47 - but that
   gap has roughly halved from n=3 (was 4.36 vs 6.38). The tidy "every ManU side nearest another
   ManU side" claim from v1 does not survive the extra matches.
2. The break is the Palace 0-0: ManU_vPal's nearest neighbour is Crystal Palace (4.023 - the
   smallest distance in the whole matrix), i.e. the two sides of that low-event draw mirror each
   other's territory more than ManU-vPal resembles other ManU games. Opponents no longer
   self-cluster cleanly either (Palace and Tottenham both sit nearest a ManU side).
3. Still territorial, NOT a shape fingerprint - and the extra matches do not change this. Remove the
   centroid (3b) and the separation is essentially gone (intra 1.558 vs cross 1.684) with mixed
   ManU/opponent nearest neighbours. What repeats is WHERE ManU occupy the pitch; n=6 surfaces no
   formation-shape signal.
4. The one trait that survives cleanly: in possession ManU's line/centroid sits higher than its own
   defending block in every match, build-up in the 52-58 m band in_poss across all five - a
   consistent get-up-the-pitch-in-possession habit.
5. Counter-press is NOT the stable 0.60-0.72 habit v1 read: match-level fraction now spans 0.48-0.75
   (ManU_vTot 0.479 / regain 0.250 falls below the old floor; ManU_vPal 0.750 above it). The two 0-3
   losses carry the two lowest 5s-regain of the five (Liverpool 0.347, Tottenham 0.250) - a weak,
   unrewarded press in the heavy defeats, but read it match-level, not as a fixed signature.
6. Palace 0-0 texture: ManU pressed hardest of all here (0.750) while Palace barely counter-pressed
   (0.314, regain 0.086) and lost the ball outside their third fewest of any side (35) - a low-event
   grind where ManU carried the press and Palace sat off.
7. Liverpool 0-3 distortion (still true at n=6): the win-it-and-go attacking transition ran shallow
   vs Liverpool (trans_pos build-up 48.9 m vs 60.8 Brighton / 56.9 Fulham) and ManU sat lower and
   narrower in possession - beaten in the win-it/lose-it phase, not out-shaped.
8. Scope: southampton_manutd is excluded - its team anchor collapsed (149084 team-0 vs 670 team-1
   player rows, balance 0.004), leaving one meaningless 22-player-blob side. The corpus here is 5
   matches, so the win column is Fulham alone. Re-anchoring is a generator fix.
9. Ceilings unchanged: possession + counter-press rest on 1.6-2.7k proximity-possession samples and
   35-102 outside-third losses per side (ball-gap), and the partial broadcast view inflates absolute
   line heights ~+11m: compare across matches, not to FIFA numbers.
10. Verdict at n=6: a territorial ManU signature is still visible but weaker and no longer clean
    (4/5, intra-cross gap halved, Palace breaks to its own opponent); no shape fingerprint emerges;
    and the "stable mid/high press" habit loosens, with the two 0-3 losses lowest on regain.
