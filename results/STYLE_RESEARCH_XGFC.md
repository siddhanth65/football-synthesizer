# xG Football Club — Complete Online Catalogue

**Deliverable 1 of 3** for the 2026-07-22 style-analysis deep research (brief:
`docs/STYLE_RESEARCH_BRIEF_2026-07-22.md`). Goal: find methods better than ours for a stylistic
analysis of Manchester United (EPL 24-25) that runs on our **censored broadcast tracking**
(~11.8/22 players visible, 26.4% usable geometry, ball usable 32-52% of the match).

## Catalogue status

| Metric | Count |
|---|---|
| Articles online at thexgfootballclub.substack.com | **105** |
| Catalogued (this run) | **105** |
| Present in local archive (`football-state-of-play/docs/xg_club_articles/`, READ-ONLY) | **91** |

The tables below detail every article, grouped by applicability verdict (most useful first). The
**NEW** badge marks articles that are online but absent from the local 91-file archive — these are
the ones the local corpus never saw and are the primary reason this online pass was commissioned.

Verdict tiers:
- **usable_now** — buildable on our current pipeline outputs with no new data class.
- **adaptable** — buildable in a degraded/honest-coverage form on our data, or the concept ports.
- **needs_full_tracking** — requires full-pitch commercial tracking (25 Hz, all 22 players); out of
  reach for our censored broadcast view.
- **not_relevant** — digests/indexes, conceptual essays, or out-of-scope meta-analyses with no
  portable technique.

## The 10 most valuable articles for the ManU stylistic-analysis goal

Ranked for building "a proper understanding of United as a team" (formations, phases, game-state,
player interactions, coaching style, CV) on our censored broadcast data — cheapest/highest-fit first.

1. **[TI-029 Flow Motifs](https://thexgfootballclub.substack.com/p/ti-029-how-flow-motifs-turn-possession)** (adaptable) — the most direct "possession → *style*" characterizer, event-data only. Short 3-pass motifs on high-confidence identity segments are buildable now and speak the exact language of a style report (one-twos vs circulation).
2. **[Lane Control / Cover Shadows](https://thexgfootballclub.substack.com/p/the-defensive-actions-behind-the)** (adaptable) — best-fit off-ball defensive method; freeze-frame formulation matches our trusted-frame censoring. Answers "how do United deny space / defend as a block" — core to low-block vs high-press game-state understanding.
3. **[DxT — Dynamic Expected Threat](https://thexgfootballclub.substack.com/p/when-context-changes-so-does-threat)** (adaptable, open-access) — conditions threat on visible-player context at pass/shot moments; upgrades our static territorial view toward structure-aware threat without full tracking.
4. **[TI-030 Line-Breaking Passes](https://thexgfootballclub.substack.com/p/ti-030-line-breaking-passes-are-not)** (adaptable) — cluster visible defenders into lines and flag line-breaks on trusted frames; directly characterizes United's progression against defensive structure (and complements our planned SBR/line-breaking work).
5. **[Team-Agnostic Signatures ("The Imposter on the Pitch")](https://thexgfootballclub.substack.com/p/can-football-models-separate-player)** (adaptable) — the best *structural* match to our data (isolated per-pass freeze frames = our censoring). Separates player from team style — the right primitive for player-player interaction analysis under identity noise.
6. **[You Should Make Metrics Context-Adjusted](https://thexgfootballclub.substack.com/p/you-should-make-football-metrics)** (adaptable, NEW) — one-line possession normalization; apply to the opposition-pack defensive numbers *immediately*. Highest value-per-effort in the corpus.
7. **[TI-016 Explainable xG](https://thexgfootballclub.substack.com/p/ti-016-explainability-might-be-the)** (usable_now, NEW) — fuzzy zones are forgiving of our noisy shot locations, and the explainability goal maps 1:1 onto our grounded-pundit-report output.
8. **[TI 019 Formation vs Style Metrics](https://thexgfootballclub.substack.com/p/ti-019-why-the-most-common-formation)** (adaptable) — the framing for tying formation labels (from public data) to our style metrics; the backbone for "every formation, every structure" without needing to classify formations from censored frames.
9. **[ScoutGPT / Transfer Simulator](https://thexgfootballclub.substack.com/p/the-football-transfer-simulator-clubs)** (adaptable) — closest analog to our opponent-conditioned synthesizer; the position-token masking trick is portable to our player embeddings even though we'd train on an external corpus.
10. **[The Unfinished Revolution](https://thexgfootballclub.substack.com/p/the-unfinished-revolution-why-football)** (usable_now, NEW) — the methodological spine for our small-n regime: Bayesian shrinkage on n=6 xG and mining off-target shots, with all three pointed-to methods already local.

*Honorable mentions:* [Expanding Elo](https://thexgfootballclub.substack.com/p/expanding-elo-to-evaluate-individual)
(usable_now, box-score-only player ratings) and [The Perfect xG Model Does Not Exist](https://thexgfootballclub.substack.com/p/the-perfect-xg-model-does-not-exist)
(calibration/GAX methodology to validate whatever xG we report) — both cheap, both directly buildable.
