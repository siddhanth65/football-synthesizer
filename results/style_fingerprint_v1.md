## Honest read (10 lines)

1. Identity: yes, weakly. In the territory+shape embedding (3a) every ManU side's nearest neighbour
   is another ManU side, and mean intra-ManU distance 4.36 sits well below ManU-to-opponent 6.38 - a
   clean ManU / not-ManU split at n=3 (no significance claim).
2. Confound: the three opponents also self-cluster (each opponent's nearest neighbour is another
   opponent), so part of the split is the home/team-0 role + camera framing, not only "ManU-ness";
   the two cannot be disentangled at n=3.
3. The signature is territorial, not shape-invariant: remove the centroid (3b) and separation nearly
   vanishes (intra 1.51 vs cross 1.64) with opponents' nearest neighbours flipping to ManU sides.
   What repeats is WHERE ManU occupy the pitch, not a formation-shape fingerprint.
4. Repeatable ManU traits: in-possession line/centroid always higher than the defending block, and a
   counter-press fraction 0.60-0.72 with 5s regain 0.35-0.47 across all three - a real mid/high-press
   habit that holds match to match.
5. Liverpool 0-3, main distortion (attacking transition): after winning the ball ManU pushed to
   centroid depth 60.8m (Bri) and 56.9m (Ful) but only 48.9m vs Liverpool; trans_pos deepest-line
   55.7/51.0 -> 43.0m. The win-it-go-forward game ran ~12m shallower against Liverpool.
6. Press went flat: ManU counter-press fraction 0.686/0.722 -> 0.600 and 5s regain 0.392/0.468 ->
   0.347, both its lowest of the three - ManU pressed less and won it back less vs Liverpool.
7. In possession ManU also sat lower (in_poss centroid 52.3m vs 55.1/56.8) and narrower (width 32.4m
   vs 38.6m at Brighton) - squeezed onto less territory.
8. Liverpool's own row: fewest balls lost outside their third (58 vs 71-84) and the lowest 5s regain
   of all six sides (0.241) - they rarely coughed it up in danger and did not need to counter-press;
   control, not chaos.
9. Scope/honesty: possession + counter-press rest on 2.0-2.7k proximity-possession samples and
   58-102 outside-third losses per side (possession is defined on a minority of frames - ball-gap),
   and the partial broadcast view inflates absolute line heights ~+11m: compare across matches, not
   to absolute FIFA numbers.
10. Verdict: a repeatable ManU territorial + pressing signature shows across three matches; the 0-3
    was set apart less by shape than by a blunted attacking transition and a weaker, less-rewarded
    counter-press - ManU were outplayed in the win-it/lose-it phase, not out-shaped.
