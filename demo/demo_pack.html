05We know exactly what could be better

The honest-limits section is the ask — each limit has a measured baseline and a named path, not a hand-wave.

Visibility ceiling: ~11.8 of 22 players in frame. A broadcast never shows the whole pitch and we never invent the rest.
Path: B4 imputation plan with measured baselines — fill off-screen players under an explicit uncertainty gate, validated against the frames where they are visible.
Per-player event attribution: 1–4% of touches. Naming is hero-shot-concentrated (close-ups cluster on a handful of players), so per-player event counts are not yet trustworthy.
Path: the December attribution route — football-domain ReID / VLM close-up reader to lift uniform coverage above the same-kit margin guard.
The retraction trail is the methodology, not an embarrassment. Two team-mapping flips (Tottenham, Southampton) sat in the corpus since MW4 and were caught by our own automated possession-vs-oracle screen — then retracted loudly. tools/verify_team_mapping.py now passes 12/12 and the demo asserts both pre-fix flips are caught. Validated-or-nothing, in practice.
